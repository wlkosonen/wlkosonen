# Wladyslaw Kosonen

I'm an injection molding technician at Okartek Oy in Finland. Outside work I build software and make films, working
with AI agents, mostly Claude in Claude Code. I set the direction, make the calls and check the results, and the agents
do much of the writing and coding. Every decision is written down with its reason, and mistakes are recorded with the
correction rather than hidden. ("Lati" in the film credits is me.)

## Software

- **[The Solver](https://github.com/wlkosonen/Weighted-integer-leximin-over-a-flow-network-one-answer-computed-and-certified)**:
  an exact, deterministic fair-allocation solver for flow networks. It splits one commodity by strict priority tiers
  and weighted leximin within a tier, in integers, and certifies its answer. It grew out of a game's power system and
  outgrew it. In its final stretch.
- **[Prepper](https://github.com/wlkosonen/prepper)**: an offline-first mobile app for an emergency food supply.
  Barcode scanning, expiry tracking, and a household nutrition "survival window". Expo, React Native, TypeScript,
  SQLite.
- **[Aether Core](https://github.com/wlkosonen/aether-core)**: an AI story workshop for the ISEKAI ZERO platform. It walks a
  writer through a story package with an AI co-author (Gemini, Claude, Mistral, OpenRouter or a local Ollama model).
  React, Express, TypeScript, Docker.
